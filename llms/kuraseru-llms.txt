# KURASERU, INC. (株式会社KURASERU / クラセル)

> KURASERU is a Kobe-based Japanese technology company that builds digital-transformation (DX) software for the elderly-care industry. Founded in October 2017 by Taiki Kawahara — a former care worker and hospital medical social worker — the company's stated mission is "介護に関わる人がもっと輝く社会へ" (toward a society where people who work in care shine brighter): use technology to remove administrative burden from care work so caregivers can spend more of their time with the people they care for. The company operates in the DX-promotion and system-development space for care-related services.

## Status in the API Evangelist network

- KURASERU publishes **no public API**, no developer portal, no documentation site, and no SDKs.
- No `/.well-known/` discovery documents are served, and no `api.`, `developer.`, `docs.`, or `status.` subdomain resolves.
- This profile is therefore a **company profile**, not an API profile. It is tracked because KURASERU is a 500 Global portfolio company operating in Japanese elder-care technology, a sector where care-record and facility-matching interoperability is an emerging API concern.
- If KURASERU later exposes a partner or public API, the enrichment pipeline will pick it up on re-run.
