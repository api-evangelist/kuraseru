# Kuraseru

KURASERU, INC. (株式会社KURASERU) is a Kobe-based Japanese technology company building digital-transformation software for the elderly-care industry. Founded in October 2017 by Taiki Kawahara, a former care worker and hospital medical social worker, the company describes its business as DX promotion for care-related services and system development. Its mission is to use technology to lift the administrative burden off care work so caregivers can spend more of their time with the people they care for.

- Website: https://kuraseru.co.jp
- Founded: 2017-10-02 · Kobe, Hyogo, Japan · 7 employees (Feb 2023)
- Certification: ISO/IEC 27001 (IS 716039)
- Backed by: 500 Global
